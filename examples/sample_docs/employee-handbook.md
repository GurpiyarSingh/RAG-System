# Employee Handbook

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours, employees choose when to work, as long as they work 40 hours per week.

## Leave

### Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave pro rata. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over until March 31 of the following year.

### Sick leave

Employees can take up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Expenses

Business travel must be approved by your manager before booking. Expense claims, with receipts, must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense.

| Expense type | Daily limit |
|--------------|-------------|
| Hotel        | 180 EUR     |
| Meals        | 60 EUR      |
| Local travel | 40 EUR      |
