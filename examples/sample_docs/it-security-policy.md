# IT Security Policy

## Devices

All company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app.

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company accounts. Never reuse a company password on another website.

## Reporting incidents

Report suspected phishing emails with the "Report phishing" button in the mail client. Report lost or stolen devices to the IT service desk within 1 hour, by phone at extension 4357.
